ABOUT US

YOUR LOCAL SUNSHINE NORTH MECHANIC

Sunshine Motor Repairs & Tyres is a trusted local auto workshop based in Sunshine North, Melbourne. The team handles all mechanical and auto-electrical repairs, log book servicing on all makes and models with company stamp, roadworthy certificates, fleet servicing, and supplies and fits a wide range of tyres and rims.

Customers value the workshop for transparent pricing, clear communication, fast turnaround, and doing the job right the first time. Whether you need a quick service or a major repair, you'll get the same straight-shooting advice every time.

  • Fully insured & licensed
  • Roadworthy certificates issued on-site
  • Tyres + mechanical under one roof
  • Honest pricing, no upsell
  • Fleet servicing available
